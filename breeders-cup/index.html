How this page fills in

Pre-entries are announced Wednesday, October 21 and the final fields are drawn Monday, October 26. As each field becomes final, its race section is rebuilt with the full field and the same dual-graph pace view you see on the daily card — one graph for early pace, one for late pace, every horse ranked against the field. A race appears in full only when every horse in it has a complete pace picture; anything less stays a schedule slot with a pending note, never a half-filled guess.
